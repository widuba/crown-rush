Privacy Policy

Hidden Towers by widuba

Last updated: May 20, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Hidden Towers collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Hidden Towers app or website.

Hidden Towers is developed and operated by widuba. If you have questions about this policy or want to request account deletion, contact:

dukesybarlow.web@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

Hidden Towers collects only the information needed to create accounts, run multiplayer games, and display game progress.

Account Information

  • Email address
  • Password authentication information handled by Firebase Authentication
  • Username
  • Player ID

Game Information

  • Room codes and game/tournament codes
  • Player colors
  • Game state, board state, turns, scores, wins, losses, and match results
  • League credits, leaderboard standing, and recent game history

Analytics Information

Hidden Towers may use Google Analytics/Firebase Analytics to understand general app usage, performance, and engagement. Analytics may include general usage events and technical information, but Hidden Towers does not use analytics for advertising.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

Hidden Towers does not collect or request:

  • Precise location data
  • Contacts
  • Photos or photo library access
  • Camera access
  • Microphone access
  • Bluetooth data
  • Health data
  • Payment card information
  • Device sensor data

Hidden Towers does not show ads and does not use advertising networks.

4. Player Pass Information

The Player Pass page may display a Player ID and QR code. The Player ID comes from your Hidden Towers account profile.

If the Player Pass page includes fields such as date of birth or notes, those fields are intended for on-screen display or printing only. Hidden Towers does not intentionally store those date of birth or notes fields in Firebase.

5. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Let players create, join, and play multiplayer games
  • Display usernames, Player IDs, leagues, scores, and match history
  • Operate tournaments and Player Pass QR features
  • Improve app reliability, layout, and gameplay experience
  • Help prevent abuse, errors, and unauthorized access

6. Third-Party Services

Hidden Towers uses Google Firebase services, including:

  • Firebase Authentication for email/password account login
  • Cloud Firestore for storing account profiles, game rooms, game state, league credits, and match history
  • Google Analytics/Firebase Analytics for basic usage and performance analytics

These services are provided by Google. Information processed by Firebase and Google Analytics is also subject to Google’s applicable privacy and data processing terms.

7. Data Sharing

Hidden Towers does not sell personal information.

Some game information is shared with other players as part of normal gameplay, such as usernames, Player IDs, player colors, scores, room status, and game results.

We may disclose information if required by law, to protect the safety or integrity of the app, or to prevent abuse or unauthorized activity.

8. Children’s Privacy

Hidden Towers is available for all ages, but it is not directed specifically to children under 13.

The app only requires email, password, and username to create an account. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided information and wants it deleted, they may contact us at:

dukesybarlow.web@gmail.com

9. Data Retention

Hidden Towers keeps account and gameplay data for as long as needed to operate the app, maintain leaderboards, support game history, and provide multiplayer features.

Some game records may remain for leaderboard, tournament, anti-abuse, or app integrity purposes unless deletion is requested and legally or technically feasible.

10. Account Deletion and Data Requests

To request account deletion or deletion of personal information associated with your Hidden Towers account, email:

dukesybarlow.web@gmail.com

Please include the email address used for your account and, if possible, your username or Player ID. We may need to verify that you control the account before deleting data.

11. Security

Hidden Towers uses Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules to help protect account and game data. However, no online service can guarantee perfect security.

Users should keep their passwords private and avoid sharing account access.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. If changes are made, the “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, account deletion requests, or data requests, contact:

dukesybarlow.web@gmail.com

This policy is written for the current version of Hidden Towers. If the app later adds ads, purchases, camera access, location, push notifications, social features, or new analytics/tracking tools, this policy and the App Store privacy answers should be updated.
