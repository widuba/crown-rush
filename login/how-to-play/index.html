Hidden Towers

Hidden Towers is a fog-of-war strategy board game where players explore a hidden grid, discover towers, claim territory, upgrade towers, survive poison gas, and race to the winning score.

Goal of the Game

Score points by revealing tiles, claiming towers, upgrading towers, and controlling strong positions.

Starting the Game

  1. Create an account or log in.
  2. Create a room or join a room with a room code.
  3. Choose your player color before the game starts.
  4. When the room is full, the host starts the game.

Every player starts on a revealed tile. The rest of the board begins as hidden white tiles.

Tile Types

Hidden White Tile

Unknown space. When you move onto it, it reveals and gives you 10 points.

Empty Tile

Turns and Movement

On your turn, you can either move one space or stay where you are.

↑ Move Up↓ Move Down← Move Left→ Move RightS = Stay
  • You can only move up, down, left, or right.
  • You cannot move diagonally.
  • Only the current player can move from their device.
  • If you reveal a hidden tile, you gain 10 points.

Towers

Towers are hidden at the start. When a player reveals a tower, they claim it. Claimed towers turn into that player’s color.

Claiming Towers

  • The first player to reveal an unowned tower claims it.
  • Claiming a level 1 tower gives 50 points.
  • Enemy players can capture your tower by staying on it for 5 turns.

Upgrading Towers

If you own a tower, you can stay on it to level it up. The tower max level is 8.

Poison Gas: 2-Player Mode Only

In 2-player mode, one hidden poison gas tile is placed somewhere on the board. Once a player reveals it, poison begins spreading at the end of full rounds.

Gas RoundNew Infected Tiles
Round 1+1 tile
Round 2+1 tile
Round 3+2 tiles
Round 4+2 tiles
Round 5+3 tiles
After Round 5No new poison spreads

Infected tiles are permanent. If you step on an infected tile, then on your next turn you are randomly teleported somewhere else instead of choosing your move.

Safe Infected Lanes

Once poison is finished spreading, owned towers create safe lanes through infected areas.

  1. Every owned tower checks in a plus shape: up, down, left, and right.
  2. It scans all the way to the edge of the board.
  3. If an infected tile is found in that line, it becomes safe for that tower’s owner.
  4. The tile shows the owner’s color and letter.
  5. If multiple owners protect the same infected tile, it is safe for all of them.

These safe tiles are still dangerous to players who do not own one of the protecting towers.

Ending the Game

  • 2-player mode: first to 2000 points wins.
  • 3–4 player mode: first to 5000 points wins, or first to own 4 towers wins.
  • If everyone stays for 5 full rounds with no movement, the game ends and rankings decide the winner.

Final Ranking

Final rankings are based mainly on points. If someone wins by a special win condition, they are shown as the winner even if another player has more points.

Quick Strategy Tips

  • Reveal hidden tiles early to collect points and locate towers.
  • Do not ignore tower upgrades. High-level towers are worth a lot.
  • In 2-player mode, poison gas changes the whole map, so control towers that can create safe lanes.
  • Capturing enemy towers can be powerful, but standing still too long may let the other player score elsewhere.
  • Use safe infected lanes after gas finishes spreading to reach areas your opponent cannot safely enter.
